# Data, copyright, and research-use notice

Drug Evidence Intelligence is software. This repository intentionally does **not** include source pharmacopoeia PDFs, regulatory dossiers, HTA reports, journal articles, or a populated evidence database.

Users are responsible for obtaining and using source documents lawfully and in accordance with the terms of the relevant publisher, regulator, pharmacopoeia, or HTA body. In particular, licensed pharmacopoeial content such as USP–NF and the European Pharmacopoeia should not be redistributed through this repository unless the applicable licence explicitly permits it.

The application stores structured facts, document hashes, page references, short supporting snippets, and extraction metadata locally. It is designed for research workflows and reproducibility, not for clinical decision-making, prescribing, diagnosis, reimbursement decisions, or regulatory submission.

Automatic extraction can be wrong. Every extracted value—especially clinical effect estimates, confidence intervals, comparators, indications, and subgroup information—must be checked against the original source before scientific publication or downstream analysis.
